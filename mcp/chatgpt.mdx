## Connect after activation

Current OpenAI guidance uses ChatGPT on the web: **Plugins → plus → Add custom MCP server**. Enter `https://mcp.liftx.io/mcp`, select OAuth and use automatic client registration. Workspace policy may restrict this setup. The published-identity/CIMD option is not supported by this Liftx implementation. [Official custom MCP instructions](https://developers.openai.com/plugins/deploy/connect-chatgpt).

Follow Liftx browser sign-in and consent. Begin with `read`, confirm your account, exact linked accounts, demo/live environment, canonical instruments and grant expiry. Use `liftx_discovery` and a bounded position read to verify access. Add trading permissions only through explicit Liftx consent and fresh authentication.

Never configure a static API-key or bearer header, select anonymous authentication, or paste passwords, tokens, exchange credentials, TradingView capabilities or callback URLs into a chat. The supported connection uses personal OAuth access; shared credentials are not a substitute.

## Use trading tools deliberately

Review the exact action before approving an OPEN, modification or termination. Read/research prompts do not authorize trades. Modification requires the current snapshot/revision. Preserve the original `client_command_id`, complete payload and receipt across timeouts or reconnects; an absent response does not prove absence of effects.

The [MCP contract](/mcp/overview) describes permissions and tools. The [receipt contract](/api/commands-and-receipts) explains why acceptance, handoff and `execution_observed` do not prove completion or flat exposure.

## Reconnect or disconnect

Access tokens expire within 15 minutes and grants within 30 days. The client owns refresh; uncertain refresh requires a new browser authorization. That does not authorize a new trading command. Revoke the grant in Liftx Settings, then disconnect in ChatGPT. Client removal alone is not evidence of server revocation.

Connecting a remote server, importing skills and publishing an OpenAI directory entry are separate workflows. The Liftx Claude package is not an already-approved OpenAI plugin. [OpenAI's Claude-plugin import guide](https://developers.openai.com/plugins/guides/submit-claude-plugin) describes a separate production/OAuth/domain/review process.
